## 3. Why Bangladeshi Families Trust Keystone Overseas

### 1. Published, Transparent Fees — No Hidden Costs
We do not operate on ambiguous "packages." Our fees are written down in a formal contract before you begin.
- **Upfront File Processing Fee:** Only ৳5,000 – ৳10,000 upon document submission (covers institutional portal processing, courier, and administrative verification).
- **Service Success Fee:** Collected strictly after your visa is issued.
- **Direct University Payments:** You pay tuition fees directly to the foreign university’s official bank account via standard Bangladesh Bank student file transfers. We never touch your tuition money.

### 2. We Solve the "Study Gap" & "No IELTS" Bottlenecks
Mainstream agencies in Dhaka frequently turn away candidates with a 3+ year study gap or those who cannot clear IELTS. At Keystone Overseas:
- **Study Gap Acceptance:** We specialize in programs that welcome 3 to 8+ years of study gap through verified work experience letters and strong Statements of Purpose (SOP).
- **Medium of Instruction (MOI) Pathways:** For students whose previous college or university was taught in English, we secure official waivers eliminating the need for IELTS.
- **Keystone Academy Support:** If a language test is required, our Dhanmondi Academy provides intensive fast-track preparation to get you exam-ready within 4 weeks.

### 3. Machine-Audited Applications (Zero Silent Errors)
Every student file submitted through Keystone passes through our proprietary document verification system. We pre-screen your marksheets, bank solvency documents, police clearance, and medical certificates to ensure zero discrepancies before embassy or migration submission.
