We protect our partners' reputation rigorously:
- **Direct University Invoicing:** Students transfer tuition directly to university bank accounts through authorized Bangladesh Bank foreign exchange branches. Keystone never accepts cash tuition.
- **Strict Compliance:** Keystone Overseas operates legally under commercial trade licensing for educational consultancy and vocational training. We strictly do not engage in unauthorized labor recruitment or passport withholding.
- **Anti-Fraud Document Verification:** Our proprietary pre-submission audit ensures that fraudulent or altered documents are detected and rejected immediately, preserving the visa integrity of our entire network.
