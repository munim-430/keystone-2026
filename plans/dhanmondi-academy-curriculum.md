## 2. Course 1: IELTS 6.5 Fast-Track (4-Week Intensive)

### Target Student Profile
- HSC pass-outs (2020–2025) and Bachelor's graduates who need an immediate IELTS 6.0–6.5 for European Schengen (Hungary), Asian (Malaysia/Korea), or direct university entry.
- Students who have taken general English classes elsewhere but failed to score above 5.5.

### Weekly Modular Syllabus
- **Week 1: High-Yield Listening & Reading Hacks**
  - *Day 1:* Section 1 & 2 listening trap detection, map navigation, multiple-choice elimination techniques.
  - *Day 2:* Skimming and scanning academic texts; solving True/False/Not Given with 95% accuracy.
  - *Day 3:* Speed reading, paragraph headings matching, and time allocation drills.
- **Week 2: Band 7.0 Writing Task 1 & Task 2 Blueprints**
  - *Day 4:* Task 1 Academic overview writing, data reporting, trend comparisons (Bar, Line, Pie, Table, Process diagrams).
  - *Day 5:* Task 2 Essay archetypes (Opinion, Discussion, Problem-Solution, Direct Question). Sentence variety, linking words, and thesis generation.
  - *Day 6:* In-class timed essay writing with individual red-line feedback from the instructor.
- **Week 3: Speaking Fluency & Lexical Precision**
  - *Day 7:* Part 1 conversational fluency, avoiding one-word answers, natural idioms and colloquial markers.
  - *Day 8:* Part 2 Cue Card structure (The 1-minute brainstorm framework, 2-minute non-stop monologue delivery).
  - *Day 9:* Part 3 abstract reasoning, handling complex philosophical/social inquiries.
- **Week 4: Mock Test Sprint & Consular Alignment**
  - *Day 10:* Full-length Mock Exam 1 (British Council / IDP test simulator under strict timed exam conditions).
  - *Day 11:* Individual diagnostic performance review + customized error-remediation plan.
  - *Day 12:* Full-length Mock Exam 2 + Consultation session with Keystone Overseas counselors for university shortlisting.

## 3. Course 2: European Vocational & Language Prep (4-Week Crash Course)

### Target Student Profile
- Diploma engineers (Polytechnic graduates), study-gap holders (3 to 8+ years), and career switchers entering:
  - Romania (Preparatory Language Year "Anul Pregătitor" or Polytechnic Diplomas)
  - Cyprus (Vocational Hospitality, Business, or IT Diplomas)
  - Malaysia (Vocational Technology & Management)
  - Candidates preparing for European embassy interviews (VFS Hungary, Romania eVisa, Croatia VFS).

### Weekly Modular Syllabus
- **Week 1: Embassy & Visa Interview Mastery**
  - *Day 1:* Deconstructing standard consular questions ("Why Europe?", "Why this specific country/university?", "How is your study gap explained?").
  - *Day 2:* SOP (Statement of Purpose) alignment: Memorizing your file story so your spoken interview 100% matches your submitted papers.
  - *Day 3:* Mock 1-on-1 consular interviews with video recording and posture/body language coaching.
- **Week 2: Workplace & Academic English for Europe**
  - *Day 4:* Technical and vocational English terminology (Hospitality, Logistics, Mechanical, Electrical, IT).
  - *Day 5:* Email correspondence with European professors, campus accommodation offices, and university registrars.
  - *Day 6:* Handling financial questions: Proof of funds, sponsorship affidavits, and cost of living explanations.
- **Week 3: Introduction to Target European Languages**
  - *Day 7:* Romanian Language Fundamentals (Alphabet, pronunciation, basic survival greetings, numbers, market and transit phrases).
  - *Day 8:* Greek/Cypriot dialect basics & Malay practical phrases for daily campus and living orientation.
  - *Day 9:* Cultural norms, legal dos and don'ts for international students in Europe, part-time work hour limits, and residency registration rules.
- **Week 4: Graduation & File Transition**
  - *Day 10:* Final Consular Readiness Test: 15-minute simulated visa interview with a panel of senior counselors.
  - *Day 11:* Certificate of Completion award + issuance of institutional English Proficiency Recommendation Letter (accepted by partner colleges).
  - *Day 12:* Direct File Opening Workshop: On-desk document verification and submission to Keystone Overseas university placement.
