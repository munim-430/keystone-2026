### PURPOSE & SCOPE
The Second Party operates an educational, language, or training institution in Bangladesh. The First Party is an established global education consultancy and language academy managing direct higher education pipelines to Cyprus, Romania, Malaysia, South Korea, Hungary, and Canada.

This agreement formalizes a collaborative partnership where the Second Party refers candidates seeking international higher education, university pathway programs, or zero-IELTS European study admissions to Keystone Overseas in exchange for guaranteed tiered commissions.

### OBLIGATIONS OF KEYSTONE OVERSEAS (FIRST PARTY)
1. **Dedicated Counselor Assignment:** Provide each referred student with dedicated, senior-level counseling from documentation through pre-departure.
2. **Real-Time Partner Tracking:** Furnish the Second Party Director with immediate WhatsApp tracking notifications at each milestone (Offer Letter, Tuition Deposit, Visa Lodgement, Visa Issuance).
3. **Free In-House Seminars:** At the Second Party's request, Keystone Overseas will conduct free physical or online guest masterclasses (*"European Work-Study Pathways & Consular Defense"*) at the partner's center to help convert existing students.
4. **Prompt Payouts:** Guarantee commission transfers strictly within the agreed 24-to-48-hour SLA upon milestone verification.

---

### OBLIGATIONS OF INSTITUTIONAL PARTNER (SECOND PARTY)
1. **Candidate Referral:** Introduce students who have completed or discontinued IELTS/language training, or who possess study gaps and seek foreign admissions, to Keystone Overseas via official WhatsApp introduction.
2. **Confidentiality:** Maintain complete confidentiality regarding internal commission schedules and partner terms.
3. **Ethical Representation:** Accurately represent Keystone Overseas' partner destinations without making false legal or immigration guarantees.

---

### TERM & TERMINATION
- This agreement is effective immediately upon signing and remains valid for twelve (12) consecutive months, auto-renewing annually.
- Either party may terminate this agreement with thirty (30) days' written notice, provided all pending student commissions for active files are honored in full.
