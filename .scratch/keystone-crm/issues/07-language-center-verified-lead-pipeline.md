# 07 Language Center Verified Lead Pipeline Engine

Type: research
Status: resolved

## Question

How can Keystone convert the raw 859 nationwide coaching center database into a high-trust, continuously producing "Verified Lead Center" pipeline?

## Answer

Researched existing verification audits (`b2b_verification_and_offer_playbook.md`, `whatsapp_live_ping.py`, `verify_facebook_liveness.py`) and operational caller workflows.

Formulated the 4-Pillar Architecture to convert raw directories into an institutional Verified Lead Engine:

1. **Algorithmic Liveness & Priority Tiers**:
   - Tier A (215 Centers): WhatsApp Business Account verified with active business avatar and low latency.
   - Tier B (463 Centers): Standard active WhatsApp accounts.
   - Tier C (181 Centers): General landlines or unverified mobile numbers.
   - Callers focus exclusively on Tier A and B first, maximizing connection rates.

2. **Caller Tele-Verification Protocol**:
   - Callers qualify 3 non-negotiable data points during the 15-second opening:
     a) Exact Director / Managing Partner full name & direct WhatsApp number.
     b) Current active student batch volume (target: centers with 20+ active language candidates).
     c) Estimated study-gap and low-IELTS dropout pool.
   - Transitioning status from `cold` to `verified_partner` upon agreement to receive MOU.

3. **Inbound Student Referral Gateway (The Lead Tap)**:
   - Verified centers receive a unique partner slug (`center-XXXX`).
   - Three referral ingest routes:
     a) Director WhatsApp Fast-Drop: Forwarding candidate phone & marksheet directly to Keystone HQ helpline (`+8801941646278`).
     b) Dedicated Web Referral Gateway: Lightweight mobile form where directors register students in 30 seconds.
     c) Branded Co-Hosted Career Days: Physical or Zoom assessment seminars for partner batches.

4. **24-Hour Reciprocity Engine (The Commission Flywheel)**:
   - ৳2,000 instant referral commission remitted via bKash within 24 hours of Stage 1 (10k/20k BDT) file opening.
   - ৳15,000–৳25,000 post-visa success bonus.
   - Transparent SMS/WhatsApp payment notification sent immediately to director, reinforcing trust and driving next batch referrals.
