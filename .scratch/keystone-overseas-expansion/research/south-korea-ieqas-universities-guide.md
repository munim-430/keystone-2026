## 1. Executive Summary & Legal Framework: What is IEQAS?

The **International Education Quality Assurance System (IEQAS)**, officially designated in Korean as the **교육국제화역량 인증제** (*Education Internationalization Competency Accreditation System*), is a rigorous regulatory evaluation jointly administered by the **Ministry of Education (교육부 - MOE)** and the **Ministry of Justice (법무부 - MOJ)** of the Republic of Korea.

### Why IEQAS is the Single Most Critical Factor for Bangladeshi Applicants:
1. **Streamlined Visa Issuance (사증발급인정서 - Confirmation of Visa Issuance)**:
   - For Bangladeshi passport holders, applying to an **IEQAS-certified institution** often allows the university to sponsor a **Visa Issuance Confirmation Certificate (VIC)** directly through the **Korea Immigration Service (출입국·외국인정책본부 / HiKorea)**.
   - Once the immigration office in Korea issues the confirmation number, the **Embassy of the Republic of Korea in Dhaka** issues the visa sticker rapidly with minimal scrutiny and virtually zero refusal risk.
2. **Exemption from Excessive Financial Documentation**:
   - Students admitted to Top-Tier IEQAS universities benefit from simplified financial documentation.
   - Non-certified or restricted universities require extensive, rigid bank solvency proofs that are heavily scrutinized at the Korean Embassy in Dhaka.
3. **Protection Against Visa Bans**:
   - The Korean government strictly penalizes universities whose international student illegal stay (undocumented stay / overstay) rate exceeds 10%. Non-certified universities face sudden visa issuance freezes (*비자발급 제한 대학*), leaving applicants stranded.

## 5. Bangladeshi Student Admission & Visa Mechanics (D-2 & D-4)

### A. Academic Eligibility & Study Gap Parameters
1. **Undergraduate (Bachelor's Degree — D-2-2)**:
   - Educational Qualification: Minimum 12 years of formal education (HSC / Alim / Cambridge A-Levels / Diploma in Engineering from BTEB).
   - GPA Benchmark: Minimum GPA 3.50/5.00 in SSC and HSC (Recommended GPA 4.00+ for tuition fee discount scholarships).
   - **Study Gap Tolerance**:
     - Up to **2–3 years** post-HSC is widely accepted across IEQAS universities with an honest statement of purpose / gap justification.
     - Beyond 3 years: Requires proof of relevant work experience, computer diploma, or Dhanmondi Academy language preparation.
2. **Postgraduate (Master's / Ph.D. — D-2-3 / D-2-4)**:
   - Minimum 4-year Bachelor's degree (CGPA 2.80+ out of 4.00).
   - Study gap tolerance: **Up to 5–7+ years** if backed by professional employment records.

### B. Language Proficiency Pathways
- **Track 1: English-Taught Programs (100% English Medium)**:
  - Required Proof: IELTS Academic 5.5 to 6.5 (or TOEFL iBT 71–80).
  - Many IEQAS universities offer 30% to 50% tuition scholarships for students scoring IELTS 6.0 or higher.
- **Track 2: Korean-Taught Programs via D-4 Language Training**:
  - For students without IELTS: Enroll in an intensive 6-month or 1-year Korean Language Institute (KLI / 어학당) on a **D-4-1 visa**.
  - Attaining **TOPIK Level 3 or 4** guarantees transition into a Bachelor's degree with a 50%–70% scholarship.

### C. Financial Solvency & Bank Proof Invariants
*All financial figures reflect direct statutory living and tuition reserves required by the Korean Ministry of Justice.*

- **Capital Region (Seoul, Incheon, Gyeonggi)**:
  - Bank balance equivalent to **20,000,000 KRW** (~$15,000 USD / ~18,00,000 BDT) maintained in a student or parent account for over 30 days.
- **Regional Universities (Outside Capital Area — Busan, Daegu, Daejeon, Gwangju)**:
  - Bank balance equivalent to **16,000,000 – 18,000,000 KRW** (~$12,000 – $13,500 USD / ~15,00,000 BDT).
- **Tuition Payment Channel**: Direct bank-to-bank SWIFT international wire transfer directly to the university's corporate treasury account in Korea.
