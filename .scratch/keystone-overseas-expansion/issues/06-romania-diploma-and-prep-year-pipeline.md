# 06 - Romania Preparatory Year & Technical Diploma Pipeline

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: None

## Question
What are the official Ministry of Education admission frameworks, university lists, tuition structures, and consular visa submission routes (specifically via third-country Romanian diplomatic missions in Southeast Asia or potential Dhaka consular missions) for Bangladeshi students seeking Romanian Language Preparatory Year ("Anul Pregătitor") or technical post-secondary diplomas without IELTS and with multi-year study gaps?

## Answer
Resolved via deep primary-source research documented at [`research/romania-prep-year-findings.md`](file:///home/munim/keystone-2026/.scratch/keystone-overseas-expansion/research/romania-prep-year-findings.md):
1. **Statutory Framework (Law 199/2023 & OME 5516/2024)**: Non-EU students have a statutory right to enroll in the 1-year (60 ECTS) *Anul Pregătitor de Limba Română* with **zero IELTS/language requirement**. Graduates obtain a lifelong certificate granting direct admission into Romanian-taught engineering, IT, and medical degrees.
2. **Tuition & Key Universities**: Regulated under Government Ordinance 22/2009. Tuition ranges from **€2,200 to €2,700/year** across POLITEHNICA Bucharest (UNSTPB), Technical University of Cluj-Napoca (UTCN), West University of Timișoara (UVT), University of Bucharest (UB), and Ovidius University of Constanța.
3. **Official Consular Bypass of India**: Following MoFA & Romanian MAE official notices (Dec 8, 2025), Bangladeshi nationals applying for Long-Stay Study Visas (Type D/SD) via `evisa.mae.ro` are officially authorized to attend consular biometrics & interviews in **Kuala Lumpur (Malaysia), Bangkok (Thailand), or Hanoi (Vietnam)**, bypassing the India visa blockade.
4. **Study Gap & Hague Apostille**: Romanian Ministry of Education imposes zero statutory age/gap restrictions (gaps up to 8 years accepted with Europass CV and employment records). Following Bangladesh joining the Hague Apostille Convention (March 30, 2025), documents are authenticated digitally via `apostille.mygov.bd` without physical consular superlegalization.
