# 02 - Dual-Corridor Service Architecture, Pricing Tiers, and Milestone Ladder

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01

## Question
How should Keystone Education price the European (Romania/Balkans/Cyprus) corridor relative to its flagship South Korea service (৳120,000 paid post-visa) given that European processing incurs external upfront costs (Ministry acceptance letters, police clearance apostille, translation, embassy courier/travel)?

## Context
South Korea operations operate on zero out-of-pocket overhead for Keystone until the student reaches the visa stage. For Romania or Serbia, the Ministry of Education fee (€100–€300) and legalization costs occur before visa lodgement. If Keystone offers "100% fee after visa" without an initial document/administration milestone, Keystone risks cash-flow bleeding on uncommitted leads.

## Answer
**Resolved via Founder Decision (Milestone Model B):**

1. **Elimination of "Free" Gimmicks:** All "100% free" marketing claims and zero-commitment promises are permanently stripped from Keystone's materials to prevent attracting uncommitted leads and depleting cash.
2. **Two-Stage Transparent Pricing:**
   - **Stage 1 (Hard Third-Party Pass-Through):** Student pays actual external costs directly or via receipted deposit (৳15,000–৳25,000 covering document attestation, university application fee, ministry processing, and courier).
   - **Stage 2 (Keystone Professional Success Fee):** ৳100,000 paid strictly upon visa issuance.
3. **Founder Warranty:** If the student's visa is rejected due to no fault of their own (no fake documents), Keystone charges ৳0 agency success fee. Third-party government receipts remain verified and transparent.
