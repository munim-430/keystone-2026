# 03 - Consular & Embassy Submission Logistics Pipeline for Bangladesh Nationals

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01

## Question
What are the exact consular submission routes, appointment lead times, and biometric requirements for Bangladesh passport holders applying for long-stay study visas (D-type) to Romania, Cyprus, Serbia, and Bulgaria? Where must students physically appear (Dhaka VFS vs New Delhi Embassy vs Bangkok/Hanoi vs Turkey transit)?

## Context
Unlike South Korea which has an embassy in Dhaka, Romania, Serbia, and Bulgaria require specific regional jurisdiction handling. Keystone needs a step-by-step SOP for student visa submission so students and parents know the realistic timeline and travel requirements before signing.

## Answer
**Consular Logistics Breakdown:**

1. **Republic of Cyprus (South) — ZERO INDIA TRAVEL:**
   - CRMD Entry Permit (*Blue Paper*) issued directly in Nicosia by the Ministry of Interior upon college application.
   - Student departs directly from Dhaka (DAC) on Emirates/Qatar/flydubai via OK-to-Board (OKTB) system.
   - Medical and Temporary Residence Permit (*Pink Slip*) finalized inside Cyprus.

2. **Northern Cyprus (TRNC) — 100% DHAKA PROCESSING:**
   - University acceptance issued on deposit.
   - Turkish Transit Visa processed in Dhaka (Turkish Embassy/VAC).
   - Arrival at Ercan (ECN) via Istanbul; entry stamped at airport immigration.

3. **Romania — NEW DELHI CONSULAR MISSION:**
   - Ministry of Education Acceptance Letter (*Scrisoare de acceptare la studii*) required.
   - eVisa lodgement on `evisa.mae.ro`.
   - In-person consular interview and passport submission at Embassy of Romania in New Delhi. (Estimated lead time: 4–6 weeks).

4. **Strategic Operational Decision:**
   - Keystone will steer volume, high-gap, and non-IELTS leads to **Cyprus** because it eliminates the New Delhi visa hurdle completely.
   - Romania is reserved for applicants specifically seeking full EU/Schengen qualifications who are willing to travel to New Delhi for consular processing.
