# 01 - Research Romania & Balkans Universities Matrix for Bangladeshi Students

Type: research
Status: resolved

## Question
Which specific universities and colleges in Romania, Cyprus (Republic of Cyprus & North Cyprus), Serbia, and Bulgaria offer:
1. Valid study visas for Bangladeshi citizens with high historical issuance rates
2. Diploma (1-2 year), Bachelor's, and Master's programs
3. Admission without IELTS (via Medium of Instruction certificate or 1-year preparatory language foundation) as well as with IELTS
4. Acceptance of study/year gaps (3 to 8+ years)
5. Low to moderate tuition fees payable in installments?

## Context
Keystone Education requires a definitive, verified institutional inventory to serve Bangladeshi students who cannot meet South Korea's stringent bank balance requirements ($10,000-$16,000 locked) or who have study gaps.

## Answer
Full research completed and saved to `research/romania-balkans-universities-matrix.md`.

### Core Findings & Selection:
1. **Republic of Cyprus (South Cyprus) Private Colleges (American College, Casa College, CTL Eurocollege):**
   - **Key Advantage:** NO New Delhi trip required. The college secures the Entry Permit ("Blue Paper") directly from the Civil Registry & Migration Department (CRMD) in Nicosia. Airlines board from Dhaka (DAC) via "OK to Board" (OKTB).
   - **Programs:** 1-2 Year Diplomas & Higher Diplomas (Culinary Arts, Hospitality, Business, Computer Systems) alongside Bachelor's.
   - **English:** Accepts MOI or internal English test. No IELTS required.
   - **Study Gap:** Accepts 4 to 8+ years gap.
   - **Visa Probability:** 80%–85%.
   - **Tuition:** €4,200 – €5,500/year.

2. **Northern Cyprus (TRNC - EMU, CIU, NEU):**
   - **Key Advantage:** 100% Dhaka-based Turkish Transit Visa, entry stamp at Ercan Airport. No New Delhi trip.
   - **Programs:** 2-Year Associate Degrees & Bachelor's. CIU offers All-Inclusive Package (€4,500–€5,800/yr for tuition, hostel, and 3 meals/day).
   - **English:** Universal MOI / internal test acceptance.
   - **Study Gap:** Extreme tolerance (10+ years gap accepted).
   - **Visa Probability:** 90%–95%.

3. **Romania (Romanian-American University, Danubius University, UPB):**
   - **Key Advantage:** Full EU & Schengen (Air/Sea) status.
   - **Programs:** 1-Year Romanian Language Prep Foundation; Bachelor's & Master's.
   - **English:** MOI accepted at RAU and Danubius.
   - **Study Gap:** 5 to 8+ years accepted with CV / work experience.
   - **Visa Probability:** 70%–80% (Requires eVisa lodgement and physical interview/submission in New Delhi).
